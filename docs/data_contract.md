# Physical data contract — UCI 350

Verified against the original `Data` worksheet on 2026-09-13. There are 30,000 customer records and 25 columns: one identifier, 23 potential explanatory fields and one outcome. The first two worksheet rows are metadata labels (blank, X1–X23, Y) and source column names; neither is a customer record. The loader checks both rows exactly rather than guessing.

All source data cells in this snapshot are numeric with integral values. Canonical dtype is pandas nullable `Int64`; this is a lossless physical representation, not encoding or imputation. Blank non-target cells would be retained as null and reported. Blank targets, non-numeric/error cells, non-integral/non-finite values, missing/extra/duplicate columns and malformed headers fail explicitly. No row, code or numeric anomaly is dropped or recoded.

The following domains are **measured** from the pinned XLS. Every column has 0 true nulls in the real snapshot; special codes are not converted to null. Monetary units are NT dollars. ## Source definitions and unresolved codes

The [official UCI description](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/350/default+of+credit+card+clients) describes repayment -1 as duly paid and 1–9 as delay in months (9 means at least nine months). It does not define observed -2 or 0. Education documents 1–4, leaving observed 0/5/6 unresolved; marital status documents 1–3, leaving observed 0 unresolved. These are semantic issues, not measured true nulls. Do not infer sentinel meanings from third-party conventions. Numeric summaries of encoded categories are descriptive arithmetic, not continuous financial scales.

UCI maps the six status, statement and payment fields to September back through April 2005; the nonsequential source names PAY_0/PAY_2 are preserved in the mapping. The manifest also persists the complete mapping. Field definitions are implemented in `src/credit_risk/data/schema.py`.

## Conceptual families and unavailable inputs

Applicant/demographic fields exist, but income, employment and periodic debt-service obligations are absent. A statement amount is not total debt; the dataset cannot directly support DTI. Exposure is represented by credit limit and bill statements; payment history by monthly status and amount fields. Utilization ratios, delinquency aggregates and other derived features are not raw source columns; the implemented derived fields are specified in [feature_engineering.md](feature_engineering.md). DTI remains unavailable and unimplemented.

Phase 3 has finalized a versioned primary feature policy in [ADR 002](decisions/002-feature-policy-and-split.md). IDENTIFIER and TARGET remain in canonical data for traceability but are excluded from future predictors. DEMOGRAPHIC_REVIEW_REQUIRED and REQUIRES_DOMAIN_REVIEW preserve source-semantic flags: primary demographics are now excluded, repayment codes are preserved categorically, and negative monetary values are retained. Candidate-feature status is conditional on the scoring-time assumptions in [leakage_policy.md](leakage_policy.md).

## Phase-3 modeling layer

The raw/canonical 25-column contract is unchanged. The preparation layer selects 19 raw financial fields (limit, six statuses, six bills, six payments), adds 26 stateless derived features and constructs 39 numeric plus six categorical inputs. It never passes customer_id/default_next_month/sex/age/education/marital_status into preprocessing. ID/demographics remain in aligned review frames; target remains a separate Series. Repayment status literals retain -2/-1/0/positive distinctions, without invented meanings. Missing monetary/status history propagates conservatively into affected aggregates; the imputer fits only on train. Missing/nonpositive credit limits fail because ratios would be undefined. Full input/output names and lineage are in the aggregate feature manifest and [modeling_dataset.md](modeling_dataset.md).
